# Open Risks

> **Tier**: Current Milestone
> **Rolled at**: Milestone boundary — resolved items archived, open items carry forward
> **Updated by**: Any agent; reviewed by producer at each sprint review

---

## Purpose

Known risks and unresolved questions for the current milestone. Agents must check
this file before beginning any task that touches a listed system. If you discover
a new risk during your work, add it here immediately — do not wait for sprint review.

---

## Risk Format

```
### RISK-NNN: [One-line description]
Status: Open | In Progress | Resolved | Accepted
Filed by: [agent name]
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Affects: [systems or features at risk]
Probability: High | Medium | Low
Impact: High | Medium | Low

Description:
[What could go wrong and why]

Mitigation:
[What is being done, or what should be done, to reduce the risk]

Resolution:
[Filled in when resolved: what happened and how it was addressed]
```
